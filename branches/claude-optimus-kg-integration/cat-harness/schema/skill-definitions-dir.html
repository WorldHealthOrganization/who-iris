Description

Where the JSON workflow-skill definitions and the conventions live.

They are split BY THEME across the harnesses that own the subject: WHO SMART / FHIR in the DAK and FHIR IG layers, the content lifecycle and document authoring in the content core, mathematical papers in the science layer, BPMN/DMN authoring and the conventions in cat-harness. Each layer keeps its own skill-definitions/ inside a declared skills directory, and every reader asks this module for all of them.
